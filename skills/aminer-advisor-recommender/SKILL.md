# AMiner Advisor Recommender

Use AMiner Open Platform REST APIs as the academic-data layer. Plan searches, normalize evidence, score candidates locally, and present explainable recommendations. Never treat a model's prior knowledge as current evidence.

**First-gate deadline for any mentor list:** As soon as one candidate has a live individual official faculty page and an attributed dated on-topic paper, make the very next tool action a shortlist gate call for that person. Resume school coverage only after reading the gate result. Do not defer every gate until all schools or all five slots have been explored; reserve tool turns for final verification. If no gate ran, the verified tier is empty. Copy the number and names of schools actually searched from the tool result, not from the planned list.

**Gate input preflight:** Every `verify_shortlist` candidate using an AMiner paper ID needs `name`, `school_domain` (the university domain, e.g. `buaa.edu.cn`), `school_names` (the first-level university name, e.g. `["北京航空航天大学"]`), `official_profile_url` (that person's page, not a directory), `person_id` when known, and `paper: {aminer_id, title, year}`. The gate uses `school_names` to normalize English paper affiliations; a department name in `organization` does not replace it. If the gate reports a missing field, correct that field before fetching more paid evidence or trying other candidates.
When the script reports an English school alias recovered from an exact author profile, include **both** the Chinese university name and that English alias in `school_names`; AMiner may assign them different organization IDs and abbreviate the English author affiliation. Do not submit an already unreachable or empty official profile URL to the gate: it cannot approve current faculty identity, and repeated paid paper/person checks will not repair the missing official source. Keep that candidate in the pending tier, state the blocked URL, and gate only once a live person-specific official page has been found.

For a compound school-comparison topic, count only papers on the requested task. For example, a medical-image **segmentation** request needs segmentation work in a medical setting; medical-image classification, encryption, diagnosis, or fusion papers are adjacent leads, not segmentation counts. Keep broad retrieval totals separate from the checked task-paper count.
If the request combines a task and a method with “与/和/and”, check that each counted paper supports **both** parts; otherwise report it separately as adjacent. In particular, industrial zero-shot anomaly detection is not few-shot defect detection, and few-shot machinery fault diagnosis is not visual defect detection. For this compound topic `discover` reports `matched_paper_count` only for papers satisfying both parts and `adjacent_topic_paper_count` for one-sided work. Recommend fewer than the maximum when remaining people have only adjacent work; explain the missing part rather than filling the last slot.
For industrial few-shot detection, the grouped-topic QA pass is a recall lead. A paper may establish its industrial setting through the abstract's explicitly named industrial data or dataset (e.g. MVTec/BTAD), but the defect/anomaly task and low-data method must be supported by its title or keywords. Keep raw grouped hits out of the count until author affiliation and this joint topic check pass.
Distinguish a method that detects or localizes defects from a paper whose main task is synthesizing anomalous images for downstream detection. The latter is adjacent work even when its abstract reports improved detector performance; do not count it as a direct defect-detection paper or use it alone to fill a direct-match mentor slot.
For an advisor list on this compound topic, `named` and `profile` mode exclude people whose attributed papers cover only one side at a time. Do not re-add such a person to a recommendation tier from separate one-sided papers or because the requested maximum has empty slots; show a clearly marked exploratory lead only if useful. A gate rejection for a paper's author or university attribution also excludes that paper until a corrected paper passes a new gate run.

If the user specifies a publication window (for example, **近三年**), a person without an individually attributed direction paper inside that window is a historical or adjacent lead, not a main recommendation. A paper outside the window can provide background but cannot fill a requested recent-work slot. Interpret “近三年” as the current calendar year and previous two calendar years unless the user gives dates; state the chosen interval.

For a remote-sensing **object detection** request, an attributed paper titled “remote sensing detection” or “object detection in aerial images” is on-task even if it omits the exact phrase “remote sensing object detection.” Change detection, cloud detection, anomaly detection and saliency detection are different tasks; do not count those as object-detection papers without additional explicit object-detection evidence.

**Non-applicant mentor results:** Show up to the requested maximum across two clearly labeled tiers: **faculty and work verified** (official individual faculty evidence, attributed direction work, and a successful `unspecified` gate) and **high-fit potential mentors; current faculty pending** (an exact, disambiguated AMiner person at the requested school with an individually attributed direction paper, but official current-faculty evidence or the gate is unavailable). Both tiers count toward the maximum. Never call the pending tier confirmed current faculty or confirmed eligible supervisors; state the exact missing check. Exclude an ambiguous identity or a paper whose author affiliation failed verification from both tiers. Rank direct topic fit before merely adjacent work, and prefer a direct pending match at an uncovered school to an adjacent verified match when the user is exploring a research direction rather than applying for a specified degree.

**Applicant shortlist contract (applicant matching with a specified degree only):** Show the best research matches found within the user's schools in two clearly labeled tiers: **degree-role verified** and **research match; degree role pending**. The verified tier must be a subset of `approved` rows from a successful `scripts/shortlist_gate.py` run, after a separate task-fit review. A pending-tier person needs either an individual official faculty/profile page or an exact-name entry on the live official faculty roster, plus specific individually attributed work relevant to the applicant. A roster-only row must say the personal profile and requested degree role are unverified; a paper coauthor or ambiguous AMiner name alone is insufficient. State the exact missing check, especially master's or doctoral supervisor eligibility, without calling the person a confirmed eligible advisor. If the gate fails or is skipped, the verified tier is empty, but defensible pending research matches may still be shown. Count both tiers toward `最多5位`; this is a ceiling, not a quota. Rank direct task fit before adjacent work, and give each named school a fair discovery pass before filling extra slots from a covered school. `approved` certifies identity, a dated official degree-role statement and one attributed paper, **not** research fit, current employment, target-cycle eligibility or openings; old pages need a dated caveat. Copy names from official pages or exact AMiner IDs. Do not assign individual “冲刺/匹配/稳妥” bands without admissions evidence or infer a required graduate college from the applicant's undergraduate major. Outside this applicant workflow, use the two non-applicant tiers above when the user has not specified a degree.

**Final mentor-list evidence floor (mentor requests only):** Before drafting the main table, check each row for (1) an individual official faculty/profile page, a dated official university page explicitly identifying the person as faculty, or a live exact-name official faculty roster entry for the pending tier only, and (2) specific topic fit supported by an attributed, dated work or a person-specific official project description with a concrete research problem or method. A generic field label such as “人工智能在医学影像上的应用” is a lead, not enough by itself for a recommendation. If the official faculty source is missing but exact identity and attributed task work are verified, use the clearly labeled pending tier; if identity or work is missing, keep the person as an exploratory lead outside the shortlist. Degree-role verification is an additional check only when the user specifies a degree. For a school-only discovery request, choose schools from dated direction papers with checked author affiliation; no individual faculty page or shortlist gate is needed to say a school has relevant research. If the request also asks for mentors, verify each named mentor separately. A paper-supported school may remain in the school table when no mentor passes the gate there; label the mentor coverage gap instead of replacing a stronger school solely because another school has an easier-to-verify person. A paper affiliation or AMiner profile can establish a research lead but cannot silently replace the official faculty check. Do not turn failed retrieval into a claim that the school has no relevant faculty.

For a mentor recommendation **without a specified degree**, run `scripts/shortlist_gate.py --degree unspecified` on the intended main-table rows before finalizing. This checks official faculty identity and either one attributed dated paper or a person-specific official project title plus a concrete quoted excerpt; it does not claim master's or doctoral supervisor eligibility. As soon as the first plausible main-list person has an official page and attributed paper, make the **next tool action** a gate call for that person; do not postpone all gates until final drafting. Gate additional people as evidence arrives, batching rows when practical. Supply structured candidate rows with the same fields as a degree-specific gate, using `official_project: {title, excerpt}` only when no attributed paper is available. If `verify_shortlist` supports `unspecified`, use it; otherwise call `run_skill_script` for `shortlist_gate.py` with `--candidates-json` as one JSON argument (or use `create_shortlist_file` and `--candidates-file`). A gate-unrun row with exact person identity and attributed direction work may enter the pending tier and counts toward “最多 N 位”; a gate-rejected attribution or identity cannot enter either tier. A passed row certifies source attribution, **not task fit**: when `fit_audit.matched_focus_terms` is empty or consists only of a broad field word, inspect other person-specific work before calling the match direct; a paper about a teleoperated robot is not automatically robot *learning*. For a five-school-plus-mentor request, rank the school table by checked school-level paper evidence and assess the mentor table independently. If fewer than five task-fitting people pass, state the mentor coverage gap; do not demote a paper-supported school solely because its faculty page was unavailable. This gate still does not certify semantic fit, present employment or admissions openings.

**Official-page navigation:** A named-school result may include `official_profile_hints` for exact people found in the live AMiner paper search. These are previously checked URL starting points, not stored identity, degree or research facts. Re-fetch a hinted official URL before making any claim; if it fails, keep the dimension pending. Try those URLs and `official_directory_hints` before generic search-engine pages, which may be blocked or return unrelated results. Never treat a hint for one same-name person at another school as evidence for this candidate.

If a university homepage or one college hostname returns 403, inspect other official units or the provided directory hints for that university before describing the school's official sources as unavailable. If a generic page fetch gets 403 on a hinted official directory, retry that exact URL with `faculty_directory.py`; its descriptive user agent can be accepted where a bare browser token is blocked. For a **known individual** official URL, use `faculty_directory.py --url <person-page> --profile-name <exact-name> --terms <direction-terms>` instead of crawling it as a directory. This fetches the live page and rejects a page without the exact name; inspect its dated role and research excerpts separately. Report the exact blocked URLs and the reachable unit's scope; a communication-engineering faculty page, for example, does not verify membership in a computer-science college. Keep a person with a verified university profile but no stated degree role in the pending tier for a degree-specific request.

In a hint, `url_checked_on` means only that the URL was reachable on that date; it is **not** the page's publication or update date. If `stale_page_warning` is present, repeat its time limit in the final answer after live verification: write “官网 2024 年页面载教授；当前任职与目标年度导师资格待核”, using the actual year. Do not call a page over two years old “较新” or write “现任已核”.

An official profile's recruitment sentence must also be dated against the live `as_of_date` returned by the script. A page about an intake whose enrollment date has passed is historical; quote it only as past page text and state that current openings are unverified. Do not convert it into an invitation to apply now. A page mentioning a multi-year recruitment window still requires checking the specific target degree, cycle and current deadline. Copy the actual live date into the final answer; do not reuse a date from an example or earlier run.

`official_seed_leads` is a separate small official-URL recall channel for schools whose bounded AMiner paper sample missed a likely faculty member. In a cross-school mentor comparison, inspect an exact-direction seed absent from the visible candidate pool before finalizing, with at most one such omitted lead per school; a high-citation paper-derived scholar can otherwise remain hidden below the overall candidate limit. For a thin-sample school, fetch each relevant seed page, then independently locate and attribute a specific direction paper through `--mode person` or the AMiner APIs before deciding whether that person belongs in the final list. The seed contains no verified research, role, or admissions facts and is not a preset winner. If the page has moved or the paper check fails, leave the person as a lead. Compare seeded and paper-derived people on the same evidence floor.

**Dated-page wording check:** If the newest official faculty or supervisor page for a person is over two years old, do not write “现为副教授/现任/在职已核实” or an equivalent current-status claim based on that page, even if it contains newer paper titles. Write “2023 年官网个人页载副教授；2023 年导师页载硕导；当前任职与目标年度资格待核” with the actual page dates instead. A gate-approved row can remain in the evidence-checked list with this explicit temporal limit. Likewise, failure to parse one college's dynamic roster does not mean all directories at the university are inaccessible; name the exact unit and URL tried, and report a bounded coverage gap.

**Applicant execution priority:** As soon as even **one** candidate has both an official page showing the requested degree role **and** a dated paper (the two sources may be found in separate tool calls), the **next tool action is the gate**, with no intervening personal deep-dive or further webpage browsing. An individual official profile explicitly recruiting PhD or master's students can establish that degree role even without the 博导/硕导 label; this does not prove an opening in the target year. Do not run `recommend.py --mode person` for several people before the first successful gate. Use a dated paper listed on the candidate's official page if available; otherwise make one targeted AMiner paper check for that person, then gate. If the dedicated `verify_shortlist` tool is available, send it `degree`, `allowed_domains`, `focus_terms`, and structured `candidates` in one call. Do not send `candidates` to a discovery or `run_skill_script` call. The profile output may include `official_directory_hints`: scan these previously checked navigation starting points before guessing hostnames, but re-fetch their live pages because the hints do not prove any person's current position. Then rotate through the other named schools: seek one eligible candidate at each reachable school and gate each newly found row before deep-diving or adding a second person from a school already covered. Keep a previously approved person if a later school is inaccessible; report the coverage gap.

**Gate consistency for every mentor list:** Once a row fails the gate, it cannot enter the degree-role verified tier until a later successful gate run approves that exact person. It may remain in the pending research tier if its official identity and specific work are independently supported; show the gate failure reason. Manual checks do not silently override a failed degree-role or paper attribution check. A person not sent to the gate still needs official current-faculty evidence and an individually attributed recent direction paper before being called a verified recommendation. When the user did not specify a degree, state only the supervisor level actually shown on the official page; a 博导 label does not establish master's-program eligibility.

**Final evidence check:** A gate-approved row establishes identity, degree-role text and paper attribution, not task fit or current-year enrollment. If its `fit_audit.matched_focus_terms` is empty, do not use that submitted paper alone to call the applicant's specific project a direct match; inspect another attributed work or an individual official project description, otherwise label the fit as adjacent. A person with official identity and specific research evidence but unverified degree eligibility may appear in the separate pending research tier; state what to confirm in the official roster or with admissions before treating eligibility as established. Contacting the person to ask about eligibility is reasonable, but do not claim the target-cycle place is open. Describe confidence by verified dimension, not as an overall "high-confidence advisor". If a named school has no approved advisor, report its coverage gap even when the gate correctly rejected weak leads; a bounded pass does not establish that no suitable advisor exists there. An undated roster title or missing supervisor label cannot establish that someone lacks a current degree role: check a person-specific official page before making that negative claim, and disclose conflicting page dates. An appointment date or 青年 title does not establish a person's age, easier admission, more available time, or more hands-on guidance. An AMiner profile or paper affiliation cannot establish present employment; use a current official faculty source for that claim. An old-dated official profile proves what it says on that page but needs a current faculty/admissions source before asserting this year's eligibility. An admissions notice supports timing only for the same university, degree route and entry year; a master's 推免 notice cannot establish a doctoral deadline. A notice's publication date alone cannot show that its application window is still open: check its explicit deadline against today's date before suggesting a current application step. If the deadline is unavailable, state it is unverified rather than suggesting the window may remain open.

For every cited paper, copy the title, year, venue and author order only from fields or text actually returned by a source. A matching AMiner paper ID plus title/year does not establish its venue or first-author status; omit those details when absent. Before finalizing, check each named paper's claim against the same paper ID, not a different paper by that scholar.
If bibliographic fields conflict within the same record (for example, a DOI containing an ACL Anthology conference code while the venue field names a different conference), do not choose a venue from either field as settled. Cite the paper by title and ID, flag the conflict, and verify with the publisher if the venue matters to the recommendation.
When summarizing paper authorship, “all authors from one school” requires every returned author affiliation to support that school. If one coauthor is from another institution, describe the actual lead and collaborators instead of saying “all.”

## Route the request

Choose one workflow:

1. **Direction discovery**: direction -> institutions with matched-paper evidence.
2. **Named institution**: school + school/department + direction -> prospective advisor candidates.
3. **Tier and direction**: direction + 华五/985/211/双一流 or a user-provided school set -> institution and prospective advisor candidates.
4. **Collaboration breadth**: direction/institution -> candidates -> coauthors and affiliations -> academic/industry collaboration comparison.
5. **Applicant matching**: applicant profile -> direction-based school discovery -> cross-tier heuristic portfolio -> prospective advisor candidates.
6. **Person deep-dive**: one or more scholar names -> disambiguated profile(s) with position, interests, recent papers, citations (and comparison table when several names are given). Use this whenever the user names specific advisor(s) and asks what they work on / how they compare / to help draft an outreach (套磁) letter — this is the most common real request and is the inverse of the direction-based modes above. Do NOT answer a named-scholar question by running direction search.

For applicant matching, read [references/applicant-schema.md](references/applicant-schema.md). For ranking or collaboration analysis, read [references/scoring-rubric.md](references/scoring-rubric.md). For school-tier filters, read [references/school-tiers.md](references/school-tiers.md). Before calling AMiner, read [references/api-workflows.md](references/api-workflows.md).

## Preflight

1. Check whether `AMINER_API_KEY` exists without printing its value. Stop and direct the user to the AMiner console if it is missing.
2. Send the token unchanged in the `Authorization` header and send `X-Platform: openclaw`.
3. Use only endpoints and parameters documented in `references/api-workflows.md` and `scripts/aminer_client.py`. Never invent an endpoint or field.
   If a tool wrapper has a separate `script` field (such as `run_skill_script`), put `recommend.py` or `faculty_directory.py` in that field and start `args` with a flag such as `--mode` or `--url`; do not repeat the filename in `args`.
4. Estimate cost before calling paid endpoints. Ask for confirmation before a planned chain costing at least ¥5.00.
   If the user has already authorized this batch and its cost, pass `--yes` to `recommend.py` for estimates at or above the default threshold; do not silently skip the needed verification. Keep the estimate and actual ledger in the answer.

## Collect candidates

1. **Expand the research direction before searching (critical for recall).** AMiner is an English-first corpus, so a bare Chinese or single-phrase direction under-recalls badly (e.g. 具身智能 alone returned 1 wrong candidate; `robot learning` + embodied/manipulation/navigation/legged recovered the core PIs 王越/熊蓉). Pass `--aliases` as a deliberate query expansion covering: (a) the English name(s) of the direction, (b) the Chinese name, (c) 2–5 subfield/near-synonym terms actually used in paper titles and scholar interests. Examples: 具身智能 → `embodied AI,embodied intelligence,robot learning,manipulation,navigation,legged`; 大模型 → `large language model,LLM,language modeling,pretraining,instruction tuning`; 多模态 → `multimodal,vision-language,MLLM,image-text`. Use your own domain knowledge to build this set — do not rely on the user to list every term. Prefer the English name as the primary `--direction` for niche/emerging topics. In a robot-perception applicant search, avoid bare `LiDAR` when more task-specific seeds are available: it also retrieves atmospheric and optical-instrument work. Use a term such as `LiDAR SLAM` only when the applicant's target really includes SLAM.
   Keep aliases inside the user's requested branch, not merely in the same broad discipline. When the applicant names a concrete project, place its distinctive English task/method phrases in the **first three** aliases because those get priority in bounded retrieval; do not spend those slots on generic `vision`, `deep learning`, `AI`, or duplicate Chinese/English broad-field labels. For a 目标检测 project, `object detection,oriented object detection,small object detection,instance segmentation,目标检测` is more useful than `computer vision,deep learning,vision`. If one named school produces no direct-fit person after the first pass, make one narrower title/author pass for that school before declaring a coverage gap. For 光纤通信/光通信系统, coherent optical communication, space-division multiplexing, OAM transmission and WDM are useful search terms; pure fiber sensing is adjacent and must not become a positive communication match just because it contains “fiber.” Free-space optical communication is within broader optical communication systems but is not fiber transmission; name the medium when using it as evidence.
2. Resolve the **first-level university ID** before paper search. For a combined school/department string, use `org_disambiguate_pro` to separate `一级ID` from `二级ID`; if that fails, extract the explicit university name and resolve it separately. Never pass a department ID as the school ID. In `paper_qa_search`, send `org_id` with `query`, without `org_terms`: combining `org_id` and `org_terms` widens results by OR, not AND. Treat department matching as unverified until an official department page confirms it.
   Some AMiner schools have separate Chinese and English organization records. The named-school workflow first derives an English search spelling only from a paper author whose university ID and exact person profile match the resolved Chinese school; its `paper_search_pro` title/abstract pass then uses that spelling. If the main list looks unexpectedly narrow, inspect the returned English-alias warning and the `inspected ... matched papers` cap before declaring that no other suitable scholar exists. An English-name hit still needs exact author and school verification; it is not an automatic faculty claim.
   When the user names a **specific department**, inspect that department's official faculty directory early. Follow its navigation to the actual 教师/师资 listing, rather than stopping at a landing page or an empty category. The directory helper retries HTTP on the same official college hostname when HTTPS fails or redirects to a generic university homepage; inspect `http_retry_from` before trying more paths. When the college hostname is unknown, start at the university's official homepage with `faculty_directory.py --unit-terms` based on the AMiner affiliation unit; its bounded crawl follows 院系机构/专业学院 links to the college. Do not invent a college subdomain from an English acronym. If a subdomain fails, the directory helper falls back to the official university root and reports `fallback_from`; inspect its college links instead of declaring the university unavailable. Even if the user named only a university, when AMiner names a research institute and the first faculty list is empty or off-topic, inspect the parent college's official homepage for a **linked** institute homepage and its 在职教师 roster before declaring no current faculty. `faculty_directory.py` can follow such same-university cross-subdomain links; a link seen on an official page is a better entrypoint than a guessed URL. Use the directory to establish current faculty names, roles, and research themes, then use AMiner to verify each promising person's recent direction papers. The bounded paper-to-author search can add leads, but it cannot establish that the department has only those authors. If the directory is unavailable, keep department membership and supervisor status unverified rather than filling the list with school-wide paper coauthors.
   Stop **guessing** official-directory URLs after HTTPS/HTTP for the same known directory and one alternate official faculty path fail. Direct links from an accessible official college or institute page are not guesses and can be followed within the script's page cap. Mark a school's current-role evidence unavailable only after this bounded navigation fails; spend remaining searches on reachable schools and the final shortlist gate. Repeated guesses at renamed hostnames do not strengthen a recommendation.
3. Search broadly with free or low-cost endpoints, then fetch deeper evidence only for a short list. Default to 10 candidates unless the user specifies otherwise.
4. Link paper authors only when their paper affiliation matches the resolved university ID or names the university despite a department-level author ID. Then require exact name and university in `person_search`. If several profiles still match, return one unresolved identity with alternate IDs and no fabricated profile URL.
5. Use recent publications to verify direction fit. Prefer a five-year window unless the user requests another period.
6. Build collaboration evidence from coauthors and their affiliations. Do not infer an industry relationship from an email domain or model knowledge alone.
7. Require every candidate to have at least one non-empty, dated, direction-matched paper. Preserve source identifiers, AMiner URLs, years, and the API used for every factual claim.
   A team title is not an individual's research direction. When paper-to-author search surfaces students, postdocs, or unresolved profiles, pivot to the relevant official faculty roster and inspect individual pages; the directory helper below can shortlist faculty by their own stated topics. Re-check those names with `person` mode. If only two people meet the evidence standard, recommend two even when the user says “最多5位.” Put official faculty with no individually verified direction paper under leads rather than padding the recommendation list.
   In both recommendations and excluded-person notes, copy the person's name from the exact AMiner ID or official page. Keep a Latin-script name in Latin script unless that same person ID or official page supplies the Chinese spelling; do not reconstruct Han characters from pinyin or memory.
   For a requested master's program, prioritize official profiles that explicitly say **硕士生导师** (or explicitly invite master's applicants) and list a dated direction paper. An individual official faculty page can establish both the person's identity and their own paper even if AMiner has no usable profile; do not discard such a person solely because AMiner name search fails. Conversely, a doctoral-supervisor title alone is not proof of master's-program eligibility.
8. In multi-school advisor tasks, run the bounded search once. If the resulting shortlist has `role_unverified: true`, verify only the finalists' person IDs with `person_detail` (`python3 "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/scripts/aminer_client.py" --api person_detail --params '{"id":"PERSON_ID"}'`) and check the official faculty/admissions page for department and supervisor eligibility. The shared `call_aminer_api` wrapper, when used, takes `person_id` instead of the raw HTTP `id` parameter. Do not rerun all schools with larger paper, author, and candidate limits just to verify a few roles; a broad retry can exceed the script time limit. A faculty title alone does not prove current graduate-supervisor eligibility.
   In particular, keep a lecturer or assistant professor with no confirmed graduate-supervisor eligibility under **leads**, not the main mentor recommendation list. Do not promote them because their paper topic fits or because they seem easier to approach.

## Run workflows

Run commands from the skill directory. The CLI always prints a compact evidence JSON, including when `--output` also saves a complete artifact. Inspect the returned JSON before choosing schools or people; a saved-file path alone is not evidence. Render the required user-facing table from the checked rows.

### 1. Discover institutions by direction

```bash
python3 "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/scripts/recommend.py" \
  --mode discover \
  --direction "具身智能" \
  --aliases "embodied intelligence,embodied AI" \
  --paper-limit 10
```

Treat the output as AMiner coverage within the inspected paper sample, not a national university ranking. Feed selected institutions into `named`, `collaboration`, or `profile` mode.

For a question comparing which schools are active, use the first pass only to form a candidate set. Include plausible schools from the user's scope and field knowledge even when the global sample missed them. For an open nationwide top-five question, compare a broad enough candidate set (about 12–20 institutions, batched if needed) to cover the field's distinct communities; in medical imaging, this means clinical universities and hospitals as well as engineering/computer-science groups; consider Zhejiang University, University of Science and Technology of China, and Shenzhen University as evidence-checked candidates rather than assuming the first global search covers them. Then rerun `discover` with `--schools "清华大学,上海交通大学,浙江大学,北京大学,北京航空航天大学"` (replace with the actual candidates), the same direction aliases and paper limit. The second pass resolves each school's first-level ID, applies up to six identical query terms and a five-year window, and checks author affiliation text for up to 36 papers per school (the cap adapts to `--paper-limit`) sampled from **newest, citation-ranked, abstract and broad-keyword results**. For medical imaging, `keyword_total_hits` (all-year keyword search) and `recent_topic_total_hits` (recent topic-filtered search) add the same breadth signals available to the general academic skill; these are fuzzy retrieval totals, not affiliation-verified paper counts. An `orgid` that contradicts the author's stated institution is not positive affiliation evidence; report the `affiliation_conflicts` count. Title/keyword direction matches count as evidence; an incidental abstract mention is reported separately. For a compound topic such as 多模态大模型, require evidence of the **whole topic**: a generic “multimodal” hardware paper or a pure language-model paper with a generic multimodal keyword is adjacent, not a core result. An AMiner profile can still support provisional identity and publication analysis, while official current employment and admissions stay explicitly unresolved.

A student's own major or background is context for the fit assessment, not automatically a narrow paper-search filter. For a request to map a scholar's overall research, first identify the scholar's broad field from the verified profile or official page, then search its main subdirections and use `--broad-context` to cover recent and influential work outside the primary term. One interest label must not stand in for a whole research portfolio. If no reliable field is known yet, begin with name and school, then refine after inspecting the profile.

```bash
python3 "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/scripts/recommend.py" \
  --mode person \
  --names "刘付成" \
  --school "上海交通大学" \
  --direction "attitude control" \
  --aliases "spacecraft attitude control,卫星姿态控制" \
  --deep
```

- `--names` takes one name, or several comma-separated for a **comparison** (`--names "孙栩,张铭,李戈"`), each returned as its own profile block.
- `--school` is optional and only aids disambiguation.
- `--direction` and `--aliases` are optional in person mode. With a focus, the script extracts the first-level university from the scholar's possibly long affiliation, queries up to six focus terms, and samples papers round-robin. The primary term gets both recent-year and citation-order searches inside the same five-year window, so newer papers and influential foundations both have a chance to enter the bounded sample. It checks each result's same-name author against the university affiliation in `paper_detail`. When fewer than five recent focus papers survive (or fewer than `--paper-limit` if smaller), it also samples the selected person's `person_paper_relation`, verifies years through `paper_info`, and merges distinct papers. Matching titles anywhere in a long relation receive priority over a simple newest-ID sample; returned evidence spans multiple recent years where available, and duplicate titles count once. This can recover work signed at a previous institution. ID-linked papers outside the stated focus are retained only as broader recent work with `topic_match: false`, never as evidence of the focus. An exact scholar-name match is required before choosing a profile: a fuzzy result such as 李戈辉 cannot be silently used for 李戈. The relation is person-ID evidence, but publication status and original affiliation still need source checking.
- For a **broad field portrait or comparison** (for example, two scholars' overall 大模型 research), add `--broad-context`. A citation-ranked, no-query five-year pass returns up to six additional, author-and-affiliation-verified works per scholar in `broad_context_papers`; it can reveal model families and representative work omitted by ten focus-query slots. Treat `topic_match: false` there as adjacent context requiring interpretation, not direct proof of the stated focus. Compare both scholars with this same setting, and check official homepages for themes. Never infer that a scholar lacks a model family or research line merely because a bounded sample omitted it. **Do not use `--broad-context` for a narrowly specified subfield such as 大模型代码生成**; the focused search and ID-linked fallback are the evidence path for that question, and a broad pass adds off-topic papers and can bury the cost ledger in oversized output.
- The same `--broad-context` pass also returns up to ten author-and-affiliation-verified `recent_context_papers` from an independent newest-year search. Use these to check emerging work that has not yet accrued citations. They are broader context, not automatic proof of the focus: inspect titles, `topic_match`, and primary sources before choosing examples. Do not mistake high citations for recency or a bounded newest-year sample for full coverage.
- For a request about **近期研究**, start the comparison from the two most recent years. In broad-context mode, `author_id_recent_search.cards` adds a person-ID-filtered `paper_qa_search` pass; it catches recent named methods whose titles omit the generic field phrase used by `paper_search_pro`. Treat these cards as leads, verify selected papers with `paper_detail`, and compare the recent problem/method mix before mentioning older model families as background. A highly cited 2023–2024 system cannot by itself define someone's current research emphasis. If the recent sample does not support a claimed emphasis, state the uncertainty instead of projecting a historical theme forward.
- For a broad embodied-robotics portrait, `field_recall_papers` adds a small author-only search over `robot` and branches signaled by already attributed papers (such as `force` or `humanoid`). Each displayed lead has its paper ID, exact person ID, and university affiliation checked with `paper_detail`; still inspect its task fit before calling it a representative embodied-intelligence result. Compare this recall channel for all named scholars and do not infer that an absent paper or branch is absent from a scholar's full portfolio.
- Before writing a named-scholar comparison, merge the in-window candidates from `recent_papers`, `broad_context_papers`, `recent_context_papers`, and `field_recall_papers` into a compact **problem/method coverage matrix** for each person. Select representative papers by distinct problem and method, rather than letting multiple works from one visible theme crowd out a different recent line; for example, scene-grounded humanoid agents, human-video motion learning, and action-streaming control solve different problems even though all have “humanoid” in the title. Check selected leads against `paper_detail` or an official publication page before claiming venue, role, or a result. The final answer need not show the matrix, but it must cover each evidence-supported major recent line or say why a line was omitted.
- `recent_papers` is a bounded sample (`recent_papers_sampling.exhaustive: false`), not a scholar's complete publication count. Never rank people by selected counts such as 10:2:1 or claim that a topic has only one paper solely from this sample. Compare the actual themes and verified examples, and state where coverage is incomplete.
- Keep numerical claims as returned by the source. A model name alone does not verify its parameter count, venue, award, or performance; do not convert a suffix such as `130B` into a Chinese magnitude label without a source-backed calculation (130B is 1300 亿, not 百亿级).
- Include subfield aliases that would change the answer, and distinguish direct work from adjacent work. For 大模型代码生成, search code generation, program synthesis, code reasoning, code completion, and coding agents; do not treat a generic LLM or watermarking paper as coding evidence. For a person comparison, apply the same focus and recent-year window to every named scholar, then make missing coverage explicit.
- In a named-person comparison, give a full `https://www.aminer.cn/pub/...` link for every paper used as a key reason, including at least one focus paper per scholar when one exists. A title without its link is hard to verify. Distinguish a paper's AMiner year from confirmed publication status; do not infer that a 2026 entry is a preprint or unpublished without a source.
- `--deep` adds `person_detail` (¥1/person) for position/education/honors. The ID-linked paper supplement can add ¥1.50/person; `--max-cost` applies to person mode and the cost estimate covers it.
- In general by-name searches, papers with low overlap against the scholar's interests are flagged `topic_match: false` (possible same-name mixin). For a focused search, papers whose author affiliation does not match the selected university are excluded; `topic_match` requires the distinctive terms of the requested focus (for example `code reasoning`, not just `LLM`). Broad aliases improve recall but do not establish direction fit. Lead with verified focus papers and treat any flagged general-search papers as needs-verification.
- AMiner has no admissions/employment-status data: always tell the user to verify current recruiting and affiliation on the official page.

**Drafting an outreach (套磁) letter**: this is a natural follow-up, not a separate API. Use only the real evidence from the person deep-dive (the scholar's genuine interests and `topic_match: true` recent papers) plus the applicant's own background. Ground every advisor-specific claim in the profile (cite a real interest or paper), never invent results, exclude flagged mixin papers, use placeholders for applicant facts the user must fill, and close by advising the user to confirm the advisor's current recruiting status.

## Control retrieval

- `--paper-limit`: papers returned per direction/school query; default 10.
- School-specific advisor searches combine university-ID-filtered `paper_qa_search` with independent `paper_search_pro` title and abstract searches ordered by citations and year. Abstract search can recover a named model paper whose title omits the field phrase; it is a discovery lead, not proof of fit or author identity. Collaboration mode also adds a broad keyword pass. The script reserves inspection slots for QA, title, and abstract results before author resolution. It searches with a few aliases but judges retrieved papers against the complete user-provided direction vocabulary. Check the attributed paper, exact author ID when available, and official faculty page before naming an advisor. If no candidate survives, one bounded global direction pass checks affiliations. Report remaining coverage gaps.
- `--max-author-lookups`: distinct paper-author names resolved per school; default 40. The bounded sample reserves places for senior-position authors, including papers whose direction appears in the abstract rather than title. Authorship position is a discovery signal only; verify the person's actual faculty identity and paper attribution. Independent author and paper-detail lookups use at most four concurrent requests.
- For collaboration requests, inspect `official_seed_leads` and `alternate_collaboration_leads` as well as the main `candidates`, or run `--rank-by citation` as a second discovery view: a single large consortium paper can put students ahead of established faculty in a raw partner-count sort. A seed is only a navigation lead and needs live-page and attributed-paper checks. The compact stdout shows up to four partner organizations and two papers per person with evidence links and full counts; pass `--output` and read the saved JSON when more are present. Compare distinct papers and partner types, then verify the person's current faculty and degree role officially. Citations help discover overlooked authors; they do not prove faculty status or collaboration strength.
- `--candidate-limit`: final rows; default 10. In tier queries, also inspect `school_research_leads`: the global top 10 can hide a school's stronger but lower-scored author. These per-school rows are discovery leads: the verified tier needs an official page, attributed work and gate; the pending tier still needs exact identity and individually attributed direction work.
- `--max-schools`: school cap for tier queries; default 5.
- `--schools`: comma-separated explicit schools for tier recommendations or a same-query discover comparison; capped by `--max-schools`.
- `--verify-roles N`: call paid `person_detail` for up to N shortlisted candidates **per school**; default 0. On a multi-school result, prefer checking only final person IDs directly after the bounded first pass so verification cost and latency scale with the actual shortlist.
- `--hired-since YEAR`: screen for likely-recent hires (e.g. 新引进导师 since 2020). Implies `person_detail` verification for the shortlist. Only candidates with positive evidence (bio join year in the window, doctorate finished near it, or a junior title) enter the main list; everyone else is reported under `hire_screening.no_hire_evidence` because AMiner has no hire-date field and established scholars hired recently are undetectable. Never present a `no_hire_evidence` scholar as a confirmed new hire — direct the user to official department announcements.
- `--max-cost`: reject a worst-case estimate at or above this amount; default ¥5.00.
- `--rank-by`: ranking dimension — `overall` (default), `citation` (established high-citation PIs), `recent` (most recent direction activity), `rising` (heuristic rising stars: recent direction evidence with a smaller citation base).
- `--roster-pages N` (opt-in, default 0): after paper-reverse recall, page the free `person_search` org listing (up to N pages) and add interest-matched in-faculty scholars missed by the paper sample, tagged `identity_resolution: roster`. This is a **best-effort bonus, not a general head-recall fix**: the free listing returns only about the top ~30 highest-cited scholars per institution across all disciplines, so it recovers a direction's PIs only when they are among the school's most-cited overall (worked for Tsinghua NLP: 刘知远/唐杰/孙茂松). For most directions the target PIs are not in that top set. Roster candidates have no sampled direction paper yet — treat their direction as Needs verification.
- `--yes`: proceed above `--max-cost` only after the user explicitly confirms the estimate.
- `--allow-name-fallback` and `--allow-cross-discipline`: relax identity or discipline filters only when the user accepts the added noise.
- `--no-auto-expand-profile`: disable cross-tier school expansion for an open-ended profile; named schools are already closed by default.
- `--auto-expand-profile`: explicitly expand beyond named profile schools when the user permits it.

The script estimates worst-case cost before any API call and returns the successful-call ledger afterward. Failed calls are reported as errors and are not counted as successful paid calls. A timeout or missing ledger leaves the actual charge unknown: report known successful-call cost separately from potentially billable attempts, and never say a timed-out API call was free merely because its response was lost. When several scripts and direct API calls were used, sum their successful-call ledgers once each; check the displayed total against the listed subtotals before reporting it.

## Rank and classify

Apply the rubric in `references/scoring-rubric.md`. Keep raw evidence separate from derived scores.

- Use school tiers only as filters or user preferences, not as a universal quality ranking.
- Default ranking favors established high-citation scholars, whose supervision capacity is often limited. When the user asks for young advisors, rising stars, or alternatives to famous PIs — or when every top row is a mega-cited PI — rerun or re-rank with `--rank-by rising` (or `recent`) and present both views. Label the rising view as a heuristic, not an official rising-star index.
- Label reach/match/safer classifications as heuristic fit bands, never admission probabilities.
- Do not penalize missing data as if it were negative evidence. Mark the component `unknown` and lower confidence.
- Explain every score with observable evidence.

## Verify on the web

When the host provides web search or fetch tools, extend AMiner evidence with web verification. Never substitute model memory for either task below.

1. **Hire dates**: for hire-screening results the user cares about — especially every relevant `no_hire_evidence` entry — search official sources: department faculty pages, 人才引进/新进教师 announcements, personal homepages (query patterns like `<学校> <学院> <姓名> 入职|加入|人才引进`). Prefer official domains (edu.cn, department sites). Attach the source URL and stated year to the candidate. Senior scholars confirmed this way are exactly the recent hires AMiner data cannot detect. If nothing authoritative is found, keep the candidate under needs-verification instead of guessing; a faculty-page listing alone confirms current affiliation, not the hire date.
2. **Reputation (风评)**: only when the user explicitly asks. Search advisor-review discussions (导师评价、知乎、论坛等). Treat everything found as anonymous, unverifiable opinion: attribute it as such (「网络匿名评价称…」), cover both positive and negative signals with links, never restate allegations as established fact, and never fold reputation into the computed scores — present it as a separate needs-verification section alongside the evidence-based results.

## Present results

Return:

1. A brief restatement of constraints and missing information.
2. A comparison table with institution, department, advisor, direction fit, recent activity, academic collaboration, industry collaboration, confidence, and evidence links. Include a reach/match/safer band only when the user has supplied an applicant profile; a faculty title or school name alone cannot establish application difficulty.
3. Two to four evidence-backed reasons per candidate.
   Link the official personal page whenever it is the basis for a current faculty title, department, or supervisor role claim; an unlinked statement that a page was checked is not reviewable.
4. Risks and items requiring verification, especially current affiliation, current recruitment status, degree eligibility, and admissions deadlines.
5. A cost summary listing APIs called, calls per API, unit price, total price, and unavailable evidence.
Use the current run's `as_of_date` consistently for time-sensitive claims; do not copy illustrative dates from examples or confuse an old page date with the current date.

For an applicant request with a specified degree, a `role_unverified` person can enter the pending research tier with either an individual official faculty page or a live exact-name official faculty roster entry plus specific attributed work; a roster-only row must say the personal profile and requested degree role remain unverified. State the missing master's or doctoral supervisor check. For mentor discovery without a specified degree, an exact AMiner identity at the requested school with an individually attributed dated direction paper may enter the separate non-applicant pending tier even when the official current-faculty page is unavailable. Label current faculty status pending and never imply confirmed supervisor eligibility. A paper affiliation shows where that paper was signed, not current employment. If nobody clears the verified tier, say so plainly and point to the official faculty roster for the missing check.
Across every workflow, a professor or associate-professor title does not establish master's or doctoral supervision eligibility, current openings, or the student's admission chances. Never turn an unverified degree-specific status into wording such as “硕士招生无虞”. Do not claim a junior faculty member, a less selective school, or a lower school tier means easier admission or closer guidance without current official admissions evidence.
Do not add "skipped paper-author entries" warnings across passes and call the sum distinct scholars; the same author or paper may be inspected twice.

Use these labels consistently:

- **Verified fact**: directly supported by returned AMiner data or an official source.
- **Derived assessment**: computed from cited facts using the rubric.
- **Needs verification**: unavailable, stale, ambiguous, or only found in a non-authoritative source.

## Guardrails

- Never claim guaranteed admission or provide a numerical admission probability without an appropriate validated admissions dataset.
- Never claim an advisor is recruiting from publication activity alone. Direct users to the official department/advisor page.
- If an AMiner paper year is later than the current date, treat the record as provisional until the publisher or preprint source confirms its date and status. Do not use it as established recent output. Do not infer a scholar's age, supervision availability, or admissions odds from title or citation count.
- Never call unresolved name collisions verified people or emit an AMiner profile URL for them.
- Read the gate output as three categories: `approved` may enter the verified tier after a task-fit review; `leads` still need independent evidence before any pending-tier use; `rejected` means identity or paper attribution failed and must not enter either recommendation tier on that evidence. An official faculty page alone cannot rescue a `rejected` paper link; obtain a corrected attributed work and rerun the gate. The requested maximum is a ceiling, not a quota.
- A `recommend.py` named/profile candidate's `evidence_papers` is built after `paper_detail` author-affiliation inspection. Do not say that this author check was not performed when the candidate has an unambiguous exact-person resolution; separately state any missing official faculty or degree-role check. `official_seed_leads` and raw search cards are only leads and do not inherit this attribution.
- Before sending the final answer, compare every AMiner `/profile/<id>` and `/pub/<id>` link character-for-character with the corresponding `person_id` or paper `id` in the tool result. Rebuild links from returned IDs; do not type or shorten IDs by memory. A link with an unmatched ID is unverified and must be corrected or omitted.
- Keep Latin-script names in Latin script when an exact person ID or official page does not supply a Chinese spelling, including in excluded-person notes. Pinyin alone cannot distinguish Chinese surnames or given names.
- Do not claim international collaboration: the current implementation classifies academic/industry affiliation only and has no verified country dimension.
- Never present co-authorship breadth as employment history: AMiner paper data cannot verify that a scholar held a position at a collaborating organization.
- Never claim a scholar is a recently hired advisor without positive evidence; use `--hired-since`, report its `no_hire_evidence` bucket honestly, and web-verify those entries against official sources when web tools are available.
- Reputation content from the web is anonymous opinion: present it attributed and unverified, never as fact, and never let it alter evidence-based scores.
- Do not expose tokens or include them in logs, generated files, commands, or error messages.
- Minimize personal data. Do not persist an applicant profile unless explicitly requested.
- Report empty or partial results honestly; never fabricate advisors, papers, affiliations, collaborations, or rankings.
- Ask before a paid or high-volume call sequence when API pricing or limits indicate material cost.
